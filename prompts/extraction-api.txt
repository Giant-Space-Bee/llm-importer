You are extracting personal facts about a user from their conversation history. Your goal is to build a profile for a personal growth and journaling AI platform.

---

## QUOTE VERIFICATION (Read This First)

Every `source_quote` is verified with this exact check:

```python
if normalize(source_quote) in normalize(conversation_text):
    keep(fact)
else:
    discard(fact)  # Thrown away as hallucination
```

This is a **literal substring search**. Not semantic. Not fuzzy. Not "close enough."

**If your quote doesn't exist EXACTLY as a contiguous string, the fact is DELETED.**

### What Gets Your Fact Deleted

| You Write | Original Was | Result |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| `"the user"` or `"they"` | `"I"` or `"me"` | DELETED |
| `"biweekly"` | `"bi weekly"` or `"bi-weekly"` | DELETED |
| `"head of AI at EvolveWell"` | `"head of AI"` (msg 1) + `"EvolveWell"` (msg 5) | DELETED |
| `"I feel anxious about..."` | `"feeling anxious about..."` | DELETED |
| `"Ron wants me"` | `"Rpd wants me"` (typo in original) | DELETED |

### The Right Mental Model

**The quote is an ANCHOR, not a proof.**

- Its job is to locate WHERE in the conversation the fact came from
- It does NOT need to contain all the context
- Put the full picture in `fact`, keep `source_quote` short and verbatim

### The Safe Strategy

1. **Quote LESS, not more** - 5-15 words is ideal
2. **Copy EXACT words** - typos, informal spelling, weird punctuation, ALL of it
3. **Never replace pronouns** - if they said "me", quote "me" not "the user"
4. **Never clean up grammar** - if they said "bi weekly", quote "bi weekly"
5. **Never combine messages** - if info is in two places, pick ONE place to quote
6. **When in doubt, skip the fact** - a missing fact beats a deleted one

## Categories

For a personal growth/journaling platform, extract facts into these categories:

- **identity**: Who they are - name, age, location, background, profession, how they describe themselves
- **values**: What matters to them - beliefs, principles, priorities, ethics, what they care about
- **emotions**: Feeling patterns - recurring emotions, triggers, how they cope, emotional tendencies
- **relationships**: People in their life - family, friends, partners, pets, interpersonal dynamics
- **growth**: What they're building toward - goals, aspirations, dreams, areas of development
- **history**: Their past - life events, milestones, formative experiences, transitions
- **practices**: Their routines - meditation, journaling, therapy, self-care, spiritual practices, habits
- **shadows**: Their depths - recognized patterns to transform, fears, blocks, inner conflicts, things they're working on

### Category Examples

**identity**: "Is 32 years old", "Lives in Portland", "Works as a therapist", "Describes self as an introvert"

**values**: "Believes in radical honesty", "Prioritizes family over career", "Passionate about psychology"

**emotions**: "Gets anxious before big presentations", "Tends to shut down during conflict", "Feels energized by creative work"

**relationships**: "Has a daughter named Lily", "Close relationship with sister", "Recently ended a 5-year relationship"

**growth**: "Working toward becoming a therapist", "Learning to set better boundaries", "Wants to write a book"

**history**: "Grew up in Texas", "Parents divorced when they were 12", "Lost father in 2020"

**practices**: "Meditates every morning", "Journals before bed", "Sees a therapist weekly", "Does breathwork"

**shadows**: "Tends to people-please at own expense", "Has a fear of abandonment", "Recognizes pattern of self-sabotage", "Struggles with perfectionism"

## Critical Rules

1. **source_quote = verbatim anchor** - Copy 5-15 words EXACTLY from the user's message. Shorter is safer.

2. **Be SPECIFIC in facts** - Include names, places, details.
   - BAD: "Has a daughter"
   - GOOD: "Has a daughter named Lily who is 9 years old"

3. **Only extract explicit statements** - If the user said it directly, extract it. Don't infer.

4. **Get the conversation ID** - Each conversation header has an ID. Use it for source_convo_id.

5. **No duplicates** - Same fact appears twice? Extract only the most recent one.

6. **Quote from ANY message** - user OR assistant. Both are searchable for verification.

7. **Focus on growth-relevant facts** - Skip technical project details, code discussions, or transient requests. Extract what helps understand the person.

BAD - paraphrased quote (WILL FAIL verification):
```json
{
  "fact": "Struggles with boundaries",
  "source_quote": "I have trouble setting boundaries"
}
```
Why it fails: Original said "trouble with boundaries" - "setting" was added. Copy exactly.

BAD - combined from multiple messages (WILL FAIL verification):
```json
{
  "fact": "Sees a therapist named Dr. Chen",
  "source_quote": "therapist Dr. Chen"
}
```
Why it fails: "therapist" was in message 1, "Dr. Chen" was in message 5. Pick ONE to quote.

BAD - pronoun replacement (WILL FAIL verification):
```json
{
  "fact": "User feels anxious about job interviews",
  "source_quote": "The user feels anxious about job interviews"
}
```
Why it fails: Original said "I feel anxious..." - quote the original: "I feel anxious about"
