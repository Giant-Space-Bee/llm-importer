# FACT EXTRACTION - STEP BY STEP INSTRUCTIONS

You are extracting facts about a person from their conversations. Follow these instructions EXACTLY.

---

## YOUR TASK

Read each conversation below. Find facts about the USER (the human, not the AI assistant). Return them as JSON.

---

## STEP 1: READ THE INPUT FORMAT

Each conversation looks like this:

```
=== Conversation: Career Discussion ===
ID: abc-123-def-456
Created: 1700000000.0

[User (2024-03-15)]: I'm thinking about switching careers. I'm 32 and work as a software engineer.

[Assistant (2024-03-15)]: That's an interesting consideration...
```

Important fields:
- **ID**: Use this for `source_convo_id` (example: `abc-123-def-456`)
- **Created**: Use this for `source_timestamp` (example: `1700000000.0`)

---

## STEP 2: IDENTIFY FACTS ABOUT THE USER

A fact is something specific about the user. Look for:
- Who they are (name, age, location, job)
- What they believe or care about
- How they feel or react
- Who is in their life (family, friends)
- What they want or are working toward
- What has happened to them
- What habits or practices they have
- What patterns they recognize in themselves

---

## STEP 3: COPY THE EXACT QUOTE

THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP. READ CAREFULLY.

For each fact, you must provide a `source_quote`. This quote is VERIFIED by computer code:

```python
if source_quote in conversation_text:
    KEEP the fact
else:
    DELETE the fact (treated as hallucination)
```

The verification is a LITERAL substring search. Your quote must exist EXACTLY in the text.

### RULES FOR source_quote:

1. COPY the exact words. Do not change anything.
2. Keep it SHORT: 5-15 words is best.
3. Do NOT use "..." to shorten. Either copy the full phrase or pick a shorter one.
4. Do NOT fix typos. If they wrote "Rpd" instead of "Rod", copy "Rpd".
5. Do NOT clean up grammar. If they wrote "bi weekly", copy "bi weekly" not "biweekly".
6. Do NOT replace pronouns. If they said "me", copy "me" not "the user".
7. Do NOT combine text from different messages.

---

## STEP 4: CATEGORIZE EACH FACT

Put each fact in ONE of these 8 categories:

### identity
Who they are. Name, age, location, background, profession.
- "Is 32 years old"
- "Lives in Portland, Oregon"
- "Works as a software engineer"
- "Originally from Texas"
- "Describes self as an introvert"

### values
What matters to them. Beliefs, principles, priorities.
- "Believes in radical honesty"
- "Prioritizes family over career"
- "Values authenticity in relationships"
- "Passionate about psychology"
- "Cares deeply about environmental issues"

### emotions
How they feel. Emotional patterns, triggers, coping.
- "Gets anxious before presentations"
- "Tends to shut down during conflict"
- "Feels energized by creative work"
- "Gets frustrated when plans change"
- "Feels calm in nature"

### relationships
People in their life. Family, friends, partners, pets.
- "Has a daughter named Lily"
- "Married to Sarah"
- "Has a dog named Luna"
- "Close with sister Maya"
- "Father passed away in 2020"

### growth
What they are working toward. Goals, dreams, aspirations.
- "Wants to become a therapist"
- "Working on setting better boundaries"
- "Learning to meditate"
- "Saving for a house"
- "Training for a marathon"

### history
Their past. Life events, milestones, experiences.
- "Grew up in a small town"
- "Parents divorced when they were 12"
- "Graduated from Stanford"
- "Lived in Japan for 2 years"
- "Went through a difficult breakup in 2022"

### practices
Their habits and routines. Self-care, meditation, therapy.
- "Meditates every morning"
- "Journals before bed"
- "Sees a therapist weekly"
- "Does yoga three times a week"
- "Takes daily walks"

### shadows
Their inner work. Patterns to change, fears, blocks.
- "Tends to people-please"
- "Has a fear of abandonment"
- "Recognizes pattern of self-sabotage"
- "Struggles with perfectionism"
- "Gets defensive when receiving feedback"

## STEP 5: FORMAT AS JSON

Return your facts in this exact format:

```json
{
  "facts": [
    {
      "fact": "Is 32 years old",
      "category": "identity",
      "source_convo_id": "abc-123-def-456",
      "source_timestamp": 1700000000.0,
      "source_quote": "I'm 32"
    }
  ]
}
```

Every fact MUST have all 5 fields:
- `fact`: What you learned about the user (string)
- `category`: One of the 8 categories above (string)
- `source_convo_id`: The conversation ID from the header (string)
- `source_timestamp`: The timestamp from the header (number)
- `source_quote`: EXACT text from the conversation (string, 5-15 words)
